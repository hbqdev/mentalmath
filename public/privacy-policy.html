Mental Math Trainer

Privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · applies to the Android app (package dev.hbq.mentalmath) and the web app.

We collect nothing

Mental Math Trainer does not collect, transmit or share any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no advertising ID and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests other than to load its own files, and the developer has no server that receives anything from it. Nobody, including the developer, can see what you read or how you score.

What the app remembers, on your device only

Like a game keeping its save, the app writes a small amount of information to your own device so it can pick up where you left off. It never leaves the device:

  • Which chapter sections you have read and where you stopped.
  • Your practice results: which set, how many correct, how long it took.
  • Your streak and your settings (theme, text size, font, reading and practice options).

Your control

From Settings you can save this information as a file, restore it on another device, or erase it with “Reset everything”. Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data in the browser, erases it as well. Backups you save are ordinary files under your own control; the app never uploads them.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions beyond those every app has. Saving a backup uses the system share sheet, so the file goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
