Mental Math Trainer

Privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · applies to the Android app (package dev.hbq.mentalmath) and the web app.

Your data stays on your device

Mental Math Trainer keeps everything it knows about you, your reading position, practice results, streak and settings, in the local storage of the app or browser on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data, and it makes no network requests other than to load its own files.

What is stored

  • Which chapter sections you have read and where you left off.
  • Your practice attempts: which set, how many correct, how long it took.
  • Your streak and your settings (theme, text size, font, reading and practice options).

Your control

From Settings you can save all of this as a file, restore it on another device, or erase it with “Reset everything”. Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data in the browser, removes it as well. Backups you save are ordinary files under your own control; the app never uploads them.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions beyond those every app has. Saving a backup uses the system share sheet, so the file goes only where you send it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
